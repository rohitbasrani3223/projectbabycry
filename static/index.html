How It Works

A full ML pipeline — from raw audio to actionable insights

01
🎙️

Audio Input

Record or upload a WAV file. Even 3–5 seconds is enough for accurate detection.

02
📊

MFCC + Delta Extraction

40 MFCCs + Delta + Delta-Delta (120 features) are extracted using Librosa — 3× richer than basic MFCC.

03
🧠

CNN Classification

A 4-block deep CNN with GlobalAveragePooling processes features and classifies into 5 cry types with 91%+ accuracy.

04
💡

Rich Emotion Report

Get detailed advice in English & Hindi, quick tips, secondary emotion inference, and doctor warnings.

Cry Types Detected

5 primary AI-detected + 5 rule-based inferred emotional states

🤖 AI Model Detects (5 Primary)

🤢

Belly Pain

Gas, colic, or stomach cramps. Sharp, intense cry often with leg pulling.

💨

Burping

Trapped air after feeding. Short, rhythmic cry that eases when burped.

😣

Discomfort

Wet diaper, wrong temperature, tight clothing, or skin irritation.

🍼

Hungry

Classic hunger cry — rhythmic and builds in intensity over time.

😴

Tired

Overtired baby crying. Whiny, accompanied by eye rubbing & yawning.

🔍 Rule-Based Inferred (5 Secondary)

😭

Colic

Intense inconsolable crying in an otherwise healthy baby, often evenings. (3+ hrs/day)

😨

Scared / Startled

Sudden loud noise or unfamiliar environment triggering a fear response.

🥺

Lonely

Baby wants to be held and needs human connection. Completely normal!

🌀

Overstimulated

Too much noise, lights, or activity. Baby needs a calm, quiet space.

🦷

Teething Pain

Gum pain from new teeth (4–7 months). Drooling + chewing are signs.
