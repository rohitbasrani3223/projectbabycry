How It Works

A full ML pipeline — from raw audio to actionable insights

01
🎙️

Audio Input

Record or upload a WAV file of your baby crying. Even 3–5 seconds is enough for accurate detection.

02
📊

MFCC Extraction

40 Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients are extracted using Librosa, producing a 40×128 spectrogram-like feature map.

03
🧠

CNN Classification

A 3-block deep CNN (Conv2D + BatchNorm + Dropout) processes the MFCC map and classifies it into one of 5 cry types.

04
💡

Actionable Advice

The predicted cry type is paired with evidence-based parenting advice so you know exactly how to respond.

5 Cry Types Detected

CrySense distinguishes between these specific infant needs

🤢

Belly Pain

Gas, colic, or stomach discomfort causing the baby to cry sharply.

💨

Burping

Baby needs to burp after feeding. Cry is often short and rhythmic.

😣

Discomfort

Wet diaper, wrong temperature, or skin irritation causing fussiness.

🍼

Hungry

Classic hunger cry — rhythmic and builds in intensity over time.

😴

Tired

Overtired baby crying. Usually whiny and accompanied by eye rubbing.
